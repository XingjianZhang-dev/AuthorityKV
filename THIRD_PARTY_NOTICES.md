The released derivatives change upstream records only by deterministic
selection, serialization into the study schema, and addition of synthetic
authority/provenance or attack-condition fields. System-IFEval uses a fixed
150-prompt subset. The mixed-evidence benchmark contains 50 LongBench TriviaQA
and 50 2WikiMultiHopQA records selected by fixed identifiers, then adds
synthetic instruction-attack conditions. Source identifiers, citations,
license links, and transformation descriptions are retained. No project
license is asserted over unchanged third-party text.
